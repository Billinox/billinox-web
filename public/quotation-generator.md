## What Is a Quotation?

A quotation is a document that communicates the proposed price of products or services to a customer.

A quotation commonly includes:

- Business information
- Customer information
- Products or services
- Quantities
- Prices
- Discounts
- Taxes
- Total quoted amount
- Quotation number
- Quotation date
- Validity period
- Terms and conditions
- Notes

A quotation helps a customer understand what a business is proposing to provide and how much it is expected to cost.

## Quotation vs Invoice

A quotation and an invoice serve different purposes.

A quotation communicates proposed pricing before a customer commits to a purchase or service.

An invoice requests payment for products or services that have been agreed upon or provided.

A typical workflow may be:

1. A customer requests pricing.
2. The business creates a quotation.
3. The customer reviews and accepts the quotation.
4. The business provides the agreed product or service.
5. The business issues an invoice.
6. The customer makes payment.
7. The business records the payment and may provide a receipt.

Billinox supports different parts of this billing workflow.

## Quotation vs Estimate

Quotation and estimate are often used interchangeably, but businesses may use them differently.

A quotation generally presents a proposed price to a customer for specific products or services.

An estimate generally provides an approximate expected cost, particularly when the final scope or cost may change.

Businesses should communicate clearly whether the amount presented is fixed, estimated, or subject to change.

## Quotation vs Receipt

A quotation is created before a transaction is completed and communicates proposed pricing.

A receipt is generally issued after payment has been received and confirms the payment.

For example:

- Quotation: "The proposed service will cost ₦100,000."
- Invoice: "₦100,000 is due for the agreed service."
- Receipt: "₦100,000 has been received."
